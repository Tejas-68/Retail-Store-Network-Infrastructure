--- [5. SECURITY RATIONALE FOR NATIVE VLAN 666] ---
Default Cisco IOS uses VLAN 1 as the untagged native VLAN. In this architecture:
1. VLAN 1 is administratively decoupled and pruned from all trunk allowed lists.
2. VLAN 666 is defined as an unrouted blackhole VLAN and designated as the native VLAN across all trunks.
3. This neutralizes 802.1Q double-tagging (VLAN hopping) attacks, ensuring that attackers cannot inject unauthorized frames into other segments.
